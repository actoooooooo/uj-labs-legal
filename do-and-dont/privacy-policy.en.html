Privacy Policy

App: DO & DON'T ／ Provider: MODE: ANY ／ Last updated: August 18, 2026

MODE: ANY ("we") sets out below how the mobile application "DO & DON'T" (the "App") handles user information.

1. What we collect and where it is stored

Everything you enter in the App (names of habits and DON'T items, check-in history, reset history, goals, savings values, group names, reminder settings, theme and customization settings, etc.) is stored only on your device. We do not send or collect this data on any external server. No account is required.

2. Photo access

With the Pro plan you can use your own photos as the App background or as item icons. Photos are read from your photo library only when you explicitly pick one, and are copied into the App's private storage and used on your device only. They are never uploaded to us or to third parties. They are removed when you clear the background, delete the item, or uninstall the App.

3. Backups

The App can export your data to a single JSON file and restore from it. Export happens only when you request it, and the file is saved wherever you choose through your device's share sheet (cloud storage, email, etc.). Exported files are under your control; we never receive their contents.

On Android devices with automatic backup enabled, the OS may back up the App's data to your Google account. You can turn this off in your device settings.

4. Pro subscription

The paid "Pro" plan (monthly or yearly subscription) is provided through Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iOS). Payment details such as card numbers are processed by the store operator and are never collected or stored by us. The App keeps only the subscription status (active or not) on your device to unlock features.

Purchase history, cancellation and refunds follow each store's terms and procedures.

5. Advertising

The App uses Google's "AdMob" to serve ads. AdMob may automatically collect advertising identifiers (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android), IP address and device information for ad delivery.

On iOS the App may ask for tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, all features remain available but ads may be less personalized. You can change this at any time in your device settings. On Android you can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings.

6. Notifications

Only if you enable them, the App uses local notifications for reminders and goal-reached alerts. Scheduling happens entirely on your device; no server is involved. You can turn notifications off in your device settings at any time.

7. Nature of the App

The App is a self-control (lifestyle) tool that helps you keep a record of daily behavior. It does not provide medical or health diagnosis, treatment or advice.

8. Minors

The App is not aimed at any specific age group. Parents and guardians can use device settings (ad personalization limits, in-app purchase restrictions, etc.) as appropriate.

9. Deleting your data

Because all App data (entries, photo copies, settings) lives on your device, uninstalling the App deletes it completely. Backup files you exported yourself should be deleted from wherever you saved them.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be revised as laws change or features are added. Material changes will be announced on this page.
